Migration to named model workflows ​

This redesign intentionally changes the recommended interface:

Earlier workflowModel workflow
Kinetic law plus an ordered flat vectorlaw(name = value, ...) binds a KineticModel
Rebuild physical keyword settings in each routineReuse CrystallisationModel
runsimulation returns problem and solutionsolve(CrystallisationProblem(model, conditions, tspan), solver; saveat) returns solution
Separate fixed and free variantsSelect a subset in a study specification
Positional PE/MCMC/ABCDE routinesEstimationProblem then solve(problem, alg)
Prior arrays indexed by kinetic block orderNamed priors of an EstimationProblem
First concentration sample treated as C0Require mapped time-zero observations
Missing likelihood variance gets a fallbackSupplied positive variance or an error loss
Seed mesh rescaled to recover omitted massChecked support/domain and no silent rescaling
Anonymous ensemble matricesNamed joint ParameterSamples with sample IDs

The numerical solver implementations and solution accessors remain reusable. solve(remake(problem; p = values), solver) is the low-level interface for automatic differentiation and sensitivity work. The vector-assembly helpers (prepare_loss, LossSetup, run_ensemble) are internal and no longer exported. Advanced configured solver and SciML interfaces retain their distinct capabilities.

Every seed description now passes through one resolution. Lognormal and Gaussian seeds become a DistributionInitialCrystals, and mesh solvers receive exact cell integrals of its density whichever entry point built the state (model conditions, experiments, initial_state_from_characteristics or feeds). Previously experiment seeds were point-sampled on the cell centres and rescaled to the target mass.

SciML-shaped estimation ​

Estimation follows the same problem/solve pattern as simulation. An EstimationProblem replaces the study specifications and prepare_fit; it prepares the solve templates once at construction and subtypes SciMLBase.AbstractSciMLProblem. solve(problem, alg) replaces fit and dispatches on the algorithm. There are no deprecation shims.
